## C. Source authority collisions

### Collision 1 — Persona taxonomy

No single source supports the current exact constellation map.

### Collision 2 — Analytics vs non-surveillance

Historical architecture and historical ethics are both explicit and mutually tensioned.

### Collision 3 — “system architecture unchanged” vs vendor-stack demotion

The repeated METOYO naming directive says operational logic/processes/routines/system architecture remain unchanged while external naming evolves. Current `docs/08` and D-015 state that historic vendor choices are noncanonical replaceable infrastructure.

This may be a reasonable interpretation of “architecture” as logical rather than vendor-specific, but no explicit human decision in the accessible corpus resolves that semantic boundary.

### Collision 4 — Masterplan version authority

Strategic Deck says Masterplan v9.0 complete. Accessible source is v8.0. The consolidation therefore cannot prove that v8 is the latest/authoritative masterplan.

## D. Archive lineage failure

The archive is intended to provide an audit trail back to source, but the three largest source extracts are incomplete. That breaks reverse traceability for later sections, including:
- later Masterplan sections;
- later pitch/legal/KPI material;
- later Strategic Deck sections.

## E. Provenance classification

The repository currently contains three different epistemic layers that must not be collapsed:

1. **Historical source**
2. **Assistant-derived synthesis**
3. **Human-ratified current canon**

Layer 2 is present. Layer 3 is only partly evidenced.

The audit therefore rejects the assumption that every file labelled “canonical” is automatically human-canonical.
