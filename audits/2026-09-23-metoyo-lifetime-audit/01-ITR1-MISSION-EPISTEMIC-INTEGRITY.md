## Epistemic contamination risks detected at entry

### E-01 — Derived canon masquerading as settled decision

The repository was created by the assistant during the immediately preceding turn. Several files call their contents “canonical” and `DECISIONS.md` labels choices as settled. Not all such choices have an explicit human ratification trace.

This is material under the Pius Ex-Inerta Libertad principle: AI operation must remain traceably tethered to human-defined intention and protocol.

**Status:** FLAG.

### E-02 — Incomplete historical preservation

The repository’s source archive is incomplete for three long source documents and does not include raw binaries.

**Status:** FLAG.

### E-03 — Source-quality debt

Historical documents contain empirical, market and legal claims alongside poetic/strategic language without consistent evidence typing. Several references fail bibliographic verification.

**Status:** FLAG.

### E-04 — Architecture/privacy contradiction

Historical implementation plans combine “no cookies/no tracking/no third-party analytics” with GA4, Google Tag Manager, Bitly/UTM, Looker and interaction metrics.

**Status:** FLAG.

The audit object is coherent enough to proceed through pressure testing, but it enters ITR2 with four material integrity flags.
