# 00 — Canonical Identity & Naming Doctrine

## 1. Canonical identity

**METOYO** is the public-facing brand, condition, and ritual space.

**The Gift Whisperer** is the internal operating engine that runs inside METOYO. It contains the working logic, prompt architecture, relational personas, ritual grammar, sourcing logic, and execution intelligence required to transform remembered fragments into meaningful gestures.

The distinction is intentional and must be preserved.

> **METOYO names the space and condition; The Gift Whisperer runs the place.**

The Gift Whisperer can be understood as a **working dwarf**: an internal actor that performs the work without claiming the stage. It may later become a dedicated AI agent or equivalent software component, but that evolution does not change METOYO's identity.

## 2. Public tagline

> **When there is room for more.**

"Room" is not decorative language. It describes the system condition:

- room to remember before searching;
- room to interpret rather than obey;
- room for ambiguity and silence;
- room for the recipient to remain unknowable;
- room for cultural and relational difference;
- room for the user to reject, alter, or ignore the system output;
- room for the system itself to disappear.

## 3. Original engine lineage

The operational project was first developed in **July 2025** as **The Gift Whisperer**.

Its original logic included:
- memory clusters;
- relational archetypes/personas;
- prompt-based evocation;
- Trident output;
- gesture grammar;
- spatial sourcing;
- reflection after giving;
- poetic UX;
- non-commercial and anti-surveillance commitments;
- lightweight technical implementation.

In **January 2026**, METOYO became the public-facing identity and ritual frame. This was an overlay, not a replacement of the engine.

### Provenance rule

Historical files are not to be rewritten merely to substitute the name METOYO for The Gift Whisperer. Their original terminology records developmental provenance.

Use this rule:

| Context | Correct naming |

## 4. Core proposition

METOYO exists to create conditions in which a person can move from a remembered fragment to a meaningful gesture without outsourcing relational attention to a commercial recommender.

The system starts with:

> **What do you remember?**

not:

> **What should you buy?**

## 5. What METOYO is

METOYO is:
- a ritual interface for emotional presence;
- a memory-reactivation space;
- a relationally structured prompt system;
- a soft system for translating remembered detail into action;
- a framework for symbolic gifting and gesture;
- a training ground for greater confidence in giving;
- a system designed to become less necessary over time.

## 6. What METOYO refuses to become

METOYO is not:
- a marketplace;
- a CRM;
- a wishlist platform;
- a product recommendation engine;
- a behavioral advertising surface;
- a relationship-profile database;
- a growth-hacking funnel;
- a gamified emotional tool;
- a system that claims to know a person better than the people in the relationship.

## 7. Canonical principles

1. **Memory precedes the object.**
2. **Relationship defines the logic.**
3. **Gesture completes the meaning.**
4. **Invitation, not interruption.**
5. **Presence over performance.**
6. **Sensing before searching.**
7. **Minimal data, maximal meaning.**
8. **Human interpretation remains sovereign.**
9. **The output is scaffolding, not authority.**
10. **The system disappears; the gesture remains.**

## 8. Public narrative

METOYO should communicate with restraint. The project is not introduced as an AI product seeking adoption, but as a ritual condition that helps a person remember something that may already contain the seed of the gift.

The public story can therefore move through this sequence:

```text
pressure
  ↓
pause
  ↓
memory
  ↓
relation
  ↓
gesture
  ↓
encounter
  ↓
reflection
```

The intended emotional movement is from uncertainty toward presence, not from uncertainty toward consumption.

## 9. System boundaries

The public identity must never obscure the operational boundaries:

- outputs are suggestions;
- ritual language must not become coercive;
- emotionally loaded domains such as grief, trauma, reconciliation, forgiveness, estrangement, or intimate conflict require careful opt-in and tone constraints;
- the system must not imply psychological diagnosis or therapeutic authority;
- geospatial information should be optional and purpose-limited;
- reflections should remain user-controlled;
- no feature should violate the forget-by-default doctrine merely because it improves analytics or convenience.
