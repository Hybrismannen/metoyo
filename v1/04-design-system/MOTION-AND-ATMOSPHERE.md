# Motion and Atmosphere

## Objective

Motion marks transitions in attention. It must not compete for attention.

## Allowed motion

- fade/translate of 4–16px;
- slow reveal of Trident arms;
- subtle line drawing;
- controlled opacity field;
- page transition with soft crossfade;
- focus transition between ritual phases.

## Timing

- micro: 120–180ms
- standard: 220–320ms
- ritual reveal: 450–700ms
- atmospheric background: >= 8s loop if used

## Forbidden

- confetti;
- bounce;
- springy reward motion;
- infinite CTA pulse;
- streak celebrations;
- dramatic shaking on errors;
- attention-grabbing autoplay video.

## Reduced motion

When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:
- disable background drift;
- use instant or <=120ms opacity changes;
- retain state-change clarity.

## Atmosphere

Visual field may use:
- paper grain;
- very low-contrast gradients;
- translucent surfaces;
- line icons;
- restrained editorial imagery.

Atmosphere may not reduce text contrast below WCAG AA.
