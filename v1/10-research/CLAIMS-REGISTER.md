## Evidence policy

A claim can move from hypothesis to evidence-backed only when:
- exact source is verified;
- source materially supports the wording;
- population/context matches;
- date relevance is understood;
- conflicting evidence is recorded;
- citation is complete.

Poetic statements about presence, memory and ritual are not automatically empirical claims, but copy must not smuggle causal certainty through metaphor.
