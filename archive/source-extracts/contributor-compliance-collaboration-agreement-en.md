✦ CONTRIBUTOR COMPLIANCE & COLLABORATION AGREEMENT





Directive: Brand and Operational Naming

METOYO is the public-facing brand, condition, and ritual space within which this system exists.

The Gift Whisperer is the internal operational engine that runs the system inside METOYO. It functions as the working logic, prompt architecture, and execution intelligence that enables memory-driven gifting and reflective gesture.

This distinction is intentional. The operational logic, processes, routines, and system architecture described in these documents remain unchanged. Only the external naming and narrative framing evolve.

“The Gift Whisperer” should be understood as a working dwarf: an internal actor that performs the work without claiming the stage. Over time, this internal actor may evolve into a dedicated AI agent or equivalent system component. This evolution does not alter the identity, purpose, or framing of METOYO.

All references to system behavior, prompts, personas, and operational mechanics continue to apply as written. METOYO names the space and condition; The Gift Whisperer runs the place.








For: The Gift Whisperer Project
Issued by: Form & Flöde – Organisations- och Processutveckling i Sverige (Org.nr: per Allabolag listing)
Version 1.0 — July 2025



I. PURPOSE

This agreement formalizes the expectations, rights, responsibilities, and boundaries of any contributor (developer, designer, writer, researcher, or other collaborator) engaging in the creation, refinement, or deployment of The Gift Whisperer, a soft system for ritual-based emotional gifting.

The project operates under an ethos of relational integrity, data sovereignty, poetic UX, and voluntary contribution or grant-supported development.



II. NATURE OF COLLABORATION

The Gift Whisperer is:

A non-commercial, Creative Commons-based system in its prototype phase

Developed and stewarded under Form & Flöde, a Swedish organizational and process development firm

Not a marketplace, CRM, or productized consumer tool

Governed by principles of disappearing systems, non-surveillance design, and non-retained memory

Contributors agree to align with these principles throughout participation.



III. ROLES COVERED

This agreement applies to:

Software developers (front- and backend)

UX designers

AI prompt engineers

Privacy/legal advisors

Writers/editors

Artists or symbol designers

Systems architects



IV. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA HANDLING

All contributors agree to maintain confidentiality over any user, system, or design data not yet publicly shared.

No user data is ever to be stored, reused, profiled, or shared beyond the immediate ritual response pipeline unless explicitly instructed by the founder.

Any technical logs (for debugging) must be anonymized and erased after no more than 14 days.

No AI prompt input or output may be reused to train proprietary or external systems.



V. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & USE

All work produced under this agreement is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and attributed to The Gift Whisperer / Form & Flöde.

Contributors may showcase their work in portfolios, talks, and grants, provided they reference the project and adhere to its non-commercial clause.

Any intention to fork, adapt, or commercialize must be discussed with the founder for alignment and written consent.



VI. COMPENSATION & Grant Structure

At present, The Gift Whisperer is in a non-commercial phase and contributions are:

Voluntary (pro bono), or

Backed by Swedish cultural innovation grants (to be applied for with contributor participation)

Form & Flöde will act as the formal organizational host and applicant for such grants.

If/when funds are secured:

Compensation will be offered equitably, transparently, and retroactively for prior labor

Contributors will have the right to accept or refuse paid engagement



VII. ETHICAL COMMITMENT

All contributors agree to uphold:

The system’s refusal to extract or automate relational care

The tone and design ethic of ritual, slowness, and emotional precision

A collective philosophy of sincere giving, not growth hacking

This project is a cultural artifact, not a product pipeline.



VIII. TERMINATION

Either party may end collaboration at any time. Upon termination:

No proprietary claims will be made by the contributor

Attribution for past work will remain unless anonymity is requested



IX. SIGNATURE & ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By joining the project, the contributor affirms their understanding and alignment with the above.
